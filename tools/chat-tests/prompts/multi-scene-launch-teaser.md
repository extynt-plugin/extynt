Create a 15-second launch teaser for a fictional product called “Orbit”. Build it as a
sequence of three scenes and work in sensible stages so you can verify each major section
before continuing.

Scene 1, 0–4 seconds: “A clearer way to focus” appears over a dark blue field with a slow,
subtle drift and one orbiting cyan accent.

Scene 2, 4–10 seconds: transition to three feature cards—“Plan”, “Create”, and “Review”.
Bring them in one at a time with consistent spacing and timing, then give the middle card
a brief emphasis pulse.

Scene 3, 10–15 seconds: resolve to the Orbit wordmark and the line “Available now”. Use a
warm amber accent for the final reveal and fade cleanly to black at the end.

Keep the typography and motion language consistent across all three scenes. Use nested
compositions where they make the sequence easier to manage, add markers at every scene
boundary, and name layers and comps so another editor can understand the project. At the
end, summarize what structural evidence was verified and what still needs a visual or
timing judgment in After Effects.
