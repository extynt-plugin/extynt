Build a polished 12-second brand opener in three stages, and check what actually landed
before moving to the next stage.

Stage 1 — foundation: create the composition, a deep charcoal background, and a soft blue
pool of light behind the centre. Let me see that foundation first.

Stage 2 — identity: add the word “EXTYNT” in bold type with a thin amber rule beneath it.
Reveal the rule from left to right, then bring the word in with a short rise and fade.

Stage 3 — finish: add a slow camera push, a restrained glow pulse on the rule, markers for
each major beat, and a clean fade to black over the final second.

Keep the look minimal, name the layers clearly, and tell me what was verified after each stage.
