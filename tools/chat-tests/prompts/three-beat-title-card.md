Build an 8-second title card with three clear beats.

Start on a deep navy background. At 0.5 seconds, draw a thin cyan line across the middle.
At 1 second, reveal the word “NORTHBOUND” above it with a short upward fade. At 2 seconds,
bring in the subtitle “FIELD NOTES” below the line, smaller and with wider tracking. Hold
the finished card until 6.5 seconds, then fade the whole design out by 7 seconds.

Name the layers clearly and add markers for “line”, “title”, “subtitle”, and “out”.
