Make a 10-second kinetic quote card for the line “MAKE THE COMPLEX FEEL SIMPLE”.

Use bold white type on near-black. Bring in one phrase at a time—“MAKE THE”, “COMPLEX”,
then “FEEL SIMPLE”—with fast upward motion and a crisp settle. Give “COMPLEX” a cyan block
behind it that wipes on just before the word arrives. After the full line holds for two
seconds, collapse the design to a small centred lockup and fade out.

Add timeline markers for each phrase and the final collapse. Keep the layout inside safe
margins and name every layer clearly.
